Visualizations

These first two visualizations compare the average height and weight of the starting lineups of high seeds versus low seeds from the 2026 NCAA tournament. The high seeds are only the one and two seeds from the tournament, which were Duke, Michigan, Florida, Arizona, UConn, Iowa State, Houston, and Purdue. The low seeds are made up of the 15 and 16 seeds from the tournament that these one and two seeds played against in round one, which are Siena, Howard, Praire View A&M, LIU, Furman, Tennessee State, Idaho, and Queens (NC).

Analysis

The first two visualizations I produced can be used to demonstrate the shear difference in physical size and strength of the starting lineups at these power four schools compared to mid-major universities. I used a five number summary to calculate the summary statistics from these four box plots. Looking at the box plots comparing the average starter height, the median starter height is 78.8 inches compared to 77.3 inches for lower seeds. Other than the one outlier, we can clearly see that high seeds consistently have a higher average starter height. This trend is also proven for average starter weights as the median for high seeds is 215 pounds compared to 203.1 pounds for low seeds. These plots help establish the high major schools as physically dominant.
